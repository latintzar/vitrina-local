# Status

The original React shopper app was paused in May 2026. This release recovers its Discover, recipe and basket interface in a standalone demonstration. Fixtures are authored for the demo; the shops, prices and stock are fictional. Browser storage keeps each visitor's basket on their device. Checkout and other connected surfaces remain provider integrations.

The earlier Sofia waitlist and Instagram-to-cart service are separate parts of the Vitrina work. A connected deployment needs participating retailers, current inventory feeds, recipe intake and checkout services.
